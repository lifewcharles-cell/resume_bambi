About

"
BAMBI is a defining force in New York City's new underground wave an artist driven by intention, movement, and identity. Born and raised in NYC with Colombian roots, he channels raw pressure of urban and deep rhythmic upbringings "Flavor with Friction" a charged interplay of groove and aggression, control and release. For BAMBI, music is physical. Shaped by his background as both a dancer and producer, his approach is instinctively precise built on flow, tension, and impact. His sound refuses to sit still, moving between depth, break-driven momentum, and explosive intensity, always guided by feeling rather than formula. Behind the decks, balancing grit with elegance, chaos with control crafting sets that hit the body as much as the mind. His momentum has carried him across many different states in the US, while reaching overseas to Amsterdam, performing a set at ADE. He's shared stages with Julian Jeweil, Tonal Theory, Triptykh, In Verruf, Angy Kore, Dasstudach and many more while gaining support from platforms and labels like Raver Ground, Dirty Techno, Techno Inc., Insomniac, Undertone, Agape, Concept 47, and radio features on Black Room, tiny studio and beyond. More than a DJ, BAMBI is a conceptual artist using rhythm as language and tension as storytelling.
